📖 FIELD MANUAL // HOW TO SURVIVE
🎯
1. Complete All Points
Maneuver through the expanded maze to eat all pellets. Clear every point without losing all lives or barrier shields to achieve VICTORY!
⚡
2. Moving Hazard Barriers
Slow electric shock barriers patrol the key corridors. If you touch one, you lose 1 Shield and +1 Ghost spawns! Touch them 3 times and you lose!
👻
3. Slow Ghost Pursuers
Ghosts stalk you at a slow, relentless speed. Direct contact costs 1 Life (🟡). Ghosts are immune to barriers, so keep your distance!
🌀
4. Quantum Warp [T]
Eat any glowing blue point (🔵) to stock a Quantum Warp charge. Press [T] to instantly warp to the safest corridor far from all ghosts!
🚪
5. 5 Wormhole Tunnels
Use the 5 glowing neon portal gates along the boundary walls (Rows 3, 8, 13, 18, 23) to instantly transport between the left and right sides of the map.
